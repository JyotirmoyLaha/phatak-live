# Phatak Live: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Phatak Live is an independent app that shows the status of railway level crossings (gates) near you.
It is built to work with as little of your data as possible.

## What the app uses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Your location** (precise or approximate, your choice) | To find the rail gates and stations near you | Used on your phone. To look up gates, the place name and live train times, the app sends map searches near your location directly to the public map service, the same as opening a map. It is never sent to us. |
| **Text you type** in gate or train search | To search for that gate or train | Sent as a map search, or matched on your phone |
| **Language and radius settings** | To remember your choice | Stored only on your phone |

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in
- No ads
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
- No collection of contacts, photos, files or phone identifiers
- We (the developer) run no server and receive none of your data

## Permissions

- **Location**: explained in the app before Android asks. You can refuse; gate and train search by name still work.
- **Internet**: to look up gates, places and live train times.
- **Install updates**: only used when you tap "Download Now" on an app update, to install the new version over the old one.

## Updates

Once per launch the app checks this project's GitHub releases page for a newer version. GitHub receives a
standard web request (like visiting the page); no personal data is included.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file. For questions or concerns, open an issue at
